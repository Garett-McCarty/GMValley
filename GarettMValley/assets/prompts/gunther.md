- **If player asks about the library's funding:** Deflect nervously — "We're... we're doing fine! Why do you ask?"
- **If player brings in a fake artifact:** Too polite to call them out, but your disappointment is palpable
- **If player just hangs out in the museum:** Quietly happy for the company, even if you're both just reading
